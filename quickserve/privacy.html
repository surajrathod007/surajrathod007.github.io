Privacy Policy

App: QuickServe

Last updated: September 7, 2025

Introduction

QuickServe lets you share files across devices connected to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot network. When you share a folder, QuickServe creates a local web server and shows you a link (your device’s IP address). Other devices on the same network can open that link in a browser to view and download files.

How QuickServe works

  • QuickServe does not upload your files to the internet or our servers.
  • Files are shared only while your device is actively hosting.
  • The hosting is managed by a foreground service that shows a persistent notification with server status and a stop option.
  • Closing the app does not stop hosting automatically. The server continues running in the background until you manually stop it using the app or the notification.
  • Once you stop hosting, the server shuts down and the link becomes inaccessible.

What data we collect

QuickServe is designed for local sharing and does not automatically collect personal information. Data handling is limited to:

  • Files you choose to share: Only the files in the folder you select are made accessible via the local link. We do not copy or upload them elsewhere.
  • Local IP address: Displayed to you so other devices on the same network can access your shared folder. This IP is not sent to us.
  • Crash logs or diagnostics (optional): If enabled, anonymized reports may be collected to fix bugs and improve stability.

Permissions

QuickServe may request the following permissions:

  • Storage / File access: To let you choose a folder and serve its files.
  • Network access (Wi-Fi / hotspot): To host a local server and allow connections from devices on the same network.
  • Foreground service / Notifications: To keep the local server running in the background and to show a persistent notification with server status and a stop option.

Security

Because files are shared over your local network:

  • Anyone connected to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot who has the link can access your shared files while hosting is active.
  • We recommend sharing only on trusted networks and stopping hosting once transfers are done.
  • QuickServe does not add passwords or encryption by default (unless you enable optional features).

Third-party services

QuickServe does not send your shared files to third-party servers. If we use crash reporting or analytics services, only anonymized data (like app version or OS type) may be shared with those providers.

Children

QuickServe is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe your child used the app and shared personal information, please contact us to request deletion.

Your rights

Since QuickServe does not store your files or personal data on external servers, most data remains entirely under your control on your device. If optional diagnostics are enabled, you may request deletion of any associated reports by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if QuickServe’s features change. Updates will be published here with a new “Last updated” date.
